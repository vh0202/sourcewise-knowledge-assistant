# Patient Cancellation and Refund Policy

Calderwick Health · Operations · Version 3.4 · Effective 1 January 2026

This policy applies to all appointments, classes, products and memberships sold by Calderwick Health clinics. Front-desk teams use it to answer patients' questions and to decide what can be refunded on the spot.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment

### Notice required

Patients may cancel or move an appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before its start time. They can do this in the Calderwick Connect app, by telephone or at any front desk.

### Late cancellations

An appointment cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice is charged a late cancellation fee of 50% of the appointment price. The fee is taken from the card on file or added to the patient's account.

### Missed appointments

A patient who does not attend and has not told us is charged the full price of the appointment. After two missed appointments in any six-month period, future bookings must be paid for in advance.

### When the fee is waived

The front desk waives the late cancellation or missed appointment fee when the patient was prevented from attending by sudden illness, a family emergency or severe weather that closed local roads. Each patient is also entitled to one goodwill waiver in any 12-month period, no questions asked. Record the reason for every waiver in ClinicDesk.

### Arriving late

A patient who arrives up to 10 minutes late is seen for the remainder of their slot at the normal price. A patient who arrives more than 10 minutes late is treated as a late cancellation and offered the next available appointment.

### If we cancel

If a clinic has to cancel an appointment, the patient is offered the next available slot with any clinician at any Calderwick clinic. When we give less than two hours' notice, we also add a £10 credit to the patient's account as an apology.

## Refunds

### Prepaid sessions and packages

Unused sessions from a prepaid package are refunded on request within six months of purchase. Because packages are sold at a discount, sessions already used are recalculated at the standard single-session price before the balance is refunded. Packages older than six months are not refunded but can be transferred to a family member.

### Products

Supports, braces, exercise equipment and other products can be returned unopened within 28 days with the receipt for a full refund. Opened products are refunded only if they are faulty. Made-to-measure items, such as custom orthotics, cannot be returned unless faulty.

### Gift vouchers

Gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and cannot be exchanged for cash.

### How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method and reach the patient within seven working days. A refund for a cash payment of more than £50 is made by bank transfer rather than from the till.

### Who can approve a refund

| Refund value | Approved by |
|---|---|
| Up to £60 | Any front-desk team member |
| £60.01 to £300 | Clinic manager |
| Over £300 | Head of Operations |

Refunds outside this policy, however small, need the clinic manager's approval and a note in ClinicDesk explaining why.

## Care Plan memberships

New members have a 14-day cooling-off period. A member who cancels within those 14 days receives a full refund, less the standard price of any sessions already attended. After the cooling-off period, monthly fees already paid are not refunded, and cancellation follows the notice terms in the Care Plan Product FAQ.

## Insurance-funded treatment

When treatment is paid for by an insurer, cancellation fees are charged to the patient, not the insurer. Any excess the patient has paid in person is refunded under this policy; amounts paid by the insurer are returned to the insurer.

## If a patient is unhappy with a decision

Explain the policy and offer to refer the matter to the clinic manager. If the patient is still dissatisfied, give them the complaints leaflet; the Complaints Handling Procedure then applies.
