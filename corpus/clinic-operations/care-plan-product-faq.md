# Calderwick Care Plan: Product FAQ

Calderwick Health · Membership Team · Updated January 2026

The Care Plan is our monthly membership for people who want regular physiotherapy and guided exercise rather than one-off treatment. This FAQ is the reference for front-desk and membership staff answering questions from patients.

## Plans and prices

### What plans are there?

| Plan | Monthly price | Included each month | Discount on extra sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | £39 | 1 follow-up session | 10% |
| Active | £69 | 2 follow-up sessions and 1 class a week | 15% |
| Performance | £109 | 4 follow-up sessions, unlimited classes and a movement screen every 3 months | 20% |

All plans include the Calderwick Connect app and a free initial assessment when the member joins.

### How long are the sessions?

An initial assessment lasts 45 minutes and a follow-up session lasts 30 minutes. Classes last 50 minutes and have a maximum of eight people.

### What does a session cost without a plan?

The standard price is £62 for an initial assessment and £48 for a follow-up session. Classes cost £14 each.

### Can family members be added?

Yes. Up to three people living at the same address can be added to an Active or Performance plan for £25 a month each. Each added person receives one follow-up session a month and the member's discount on extra sessions.

### Is there a plan for under-18s?

Members must be 16 or over. A parent or guardian signs the agreement for members aged 16 or 17.

## Using the plan

### Where can members use their sessions?

At any of the five Calderwick clinics: Eastmere, Lindenquay, Rookfield, Westhallow and Pennybridge. Sessions can be booked with any physiotherapist.

### What happens to sessions that are not used?

Unused sessions roll over for one month. A session that is still unused at the end of the following month is lost. Classes do not roll over.

### How do members book?

In the Calderwick Connect app, by telephone or at the front desk. Members can book up to eight weeks ahead; non-members can book four weeks ahead.

### What is in the Calderwick Connect app?

Booking and rescheduling, appointment reminders, a personal exercise programme with videos set by the member's physiotherapist, and secure messages to the clinic. The app is free for members and for any patient with an active course of treatment.

## Payments

### How is the plan paid for?

By Direct Debit on the 1st of each month. If a payment fails we try again five days later; if the second attempt fails, the plan is suspended until the balance is cleared.

### Can the plan be combined with health insurance?

No. Sessions funded by an insurer are booked and billed separately, and plan discounts do not apply to them.

### Do prices change?

Prices are reviewed every April. Members are given at least 30 days' notice of any increase and may cancel without notice if they do not accept it.

## Changing, pausing and cancelling

### Is there a minimum term?

Yes, three months. After that the plan continues month to month.

### How does a member cancel?

By giving 30 days' notice in the app or in writing to any clinic, once the three-month minimum term has been completed. A member who cancels within 14 days of joining gets a refund under the Patient Cancellation and Refund Policy.

### Can a member pause the plan?

Yes. A plan can be paused for one or two whole months in any 12-month period, for example for a long holiday. No fee is charged while a plan is paused, and paused months do not count towards the minimum term.

### What if a member cannot use the plan for medical reasons?

With a note from a doctor or a Calderwick physiotherapist, a plan can be frozen for up to three months without using the normal pause allowance.

### Can a member change plan?

Members can move up to a higher plan at any time, effective from the next payment date. Moving down to a lower plan is allowed once the minimum term is complete and takes effect after one full month's notice.

## Problems

### Who handles questions about billing?

The Membership Team, weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00, at membership@example.com. Front-desk staff can view a member's payment history in ClinicDesk but cannot change Direct Debit details.
