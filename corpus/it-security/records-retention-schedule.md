# Records Retention Schedule

Calderwick Health · Information Governance · Version 2.3 · Effective 1 April 2025

## About this schedule

This schedule says how long Calderwick Health keeps each type of record and what happens to it afterwards. Keeping records for too long is as much of a risk as destroying them too early. Record owners review their holdings every January and arrange disposal of anything past its retention period.

## Patient records

| Record | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Adult clinical records | 8 years after the last appointment | Securely destroyed |
| Children's clinical records | Until the patient's 25th birthday | Securely destroyed |
| Appointment and attendance history | 8 years after the last appointment | Securely destroyed |
| Consent forms | Kept with the clinical record for the same period | Securely destroyed |
| Recordings of telephone calls to clinics | 90 days | Automatically deleted |
| CCTV footage from waiting areas | 30 days | Automatically overwritten |
| Patient complaints and responses | 10 years after the complaint is closed | Securely destroyed |

If a patient was 17 when treatment ended, the children's record is kept until their 26th birthday.

## Staff records

| Record | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Personnel files | 6 years after employment ends | Securely destroyed |
| Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates | 6 months after the vacancy closes | Deleted |
| Payroll and pension records | 6 years after the end of the tax year | Securely destroyed |
| Training records | 6 years after employment ends | Securely destroyed |
| Accident reports | 3 years after the date of the accident | Securely destroyed |
| Occupational health records | 40 years after the last entry | Securely destroyed |

## Financial and corporate records

| Record | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices, receipts and expense claims | 6 years after the end of the financial year | Securely destroyed |
| Contracts with suppliers | 6 years after the contract ends | Securely destroyed |
| Board minutes | Permanently | Transferred to the company archive |
| Insurance policies | 40 years | Securely destroyed |

## Systems and backups

| Record | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| System backups | 35 days | Automatically overwritten |
| Email in mailboxes | 2 years in the mailbox, then archived for a further 5 years | Automatically deleted |
| Security and access logs | 12 months | Automatically deleted |

## Holding records for longer

A record must not be destroyed while it is relevant to an open complaint, an insurance claim, a legal claim or a request from a regulator, even if its retention period has ended. The Data Protection Officer places a hold on the record and lifts it when the matter is closed.

## How records are destroyed

Paper records go into the locked shredding bins, which are emptied by an approved contractor who provides a certificate of destruction. Electronic records are deleted by IT using a method that prevents recovery. The record owner logs each disposal in the disposal register, giving the type of record, the date range and the date of destruction.

## Requests from patients

A patient may ask for a copy of their record at any time while we hold it. Requests are passed to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@example.com on the day they are received and are answered within one month.
