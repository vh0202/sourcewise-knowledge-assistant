IT Service Desk FAQ

Calderwick Health · IT Service Desk · Last reviewed January 2026

Answers to the questions the Service Desk hears most often. If your question is not here, raise a ticket.

Contacting the Service Desk

How do I get help?

Raise a ticket in the Service Desk portal, email it-servicedesk@example.com, or call extension 4357 from any clinic phone. Use the phone for anything that stops a clinic seeing patients.

When is the Service Desk open?

The Service Desk is staffed from 07:30 to 18:30 Monday to Friday and from 08:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays. Outside those hours, extension 4357 diverts to the on-call engineer, who deals with Priority 1 problems only.

How quickly will my ticket be handled?

Every ticket is given a priority when it is logged. Response times are measured during opening hours, except for Priority 1.

PriorityMeaningFirst responseTarget fix
Priority 1A clinic cannot see patients or take payments15 minutes, at any time4 hours
Priority 2Several people cannot work, or one clinician cannot access patient records1 hour1 working day
Priority 3One person is affected and a workaround exists4 hours3 working days
Priority 4Requests and questions, such as new software or a change of access1 working day10 working days

Accounts and passwords

I have forgotten my password. What do I do?

Go to the self-service page at the sign-in screen and choose “Forgotten password”. You will need the authenticator app on your phone. If you have also lost your phone, call the Service Desk; an engineer will verify your identity with your manager before resetting anything.

My account is locked.

Accounts lock after six failed sign-in attempts and unlock by themselves after 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, call the Service Desk.

How do I set up the authenticator app on a new phone?

Sign in to the self-service page from your laptop while you still have the old phone, choose “Security info” and add the new phone. If the old phone is gone, raise a Priority 3 ticket and we will reset your sign-in methods after an identity check.

Can I have access to a shared mailbox or folder?

Ask the owner of the mailbox or folder to raise the ticket, or attach their written approval to yours. We cannot grant access on your own request.

Working remotely

How do I connect to the VPN?

Open CalderLink from the system tray and sign in with your normal account; approve the prompt in the authenticator app. Connect before you open ClinicDesk or a shared drive. The VPN disconnects after 12 hours and whenever the laptop sleeps.

Can I read work email on my own phone?

Yes, through the Company Portal app. Install it from your phone's app store, sign in, and set the six-digit PIN it asks for. ClinicDesk is not available on personal devices.

Can I use my own computer for work?

No. Personal computers cannot connect to the VPN or to ClinicDesk.

Equipment and software

How do I order equipment for a new starter?

The hiring manager raises a New Starter request at least 10 working days before the start date. Later requests may mean the laptop is not ready on day one.

How do I request new software?

Raise a Software Request ticket describing what you need and why. Anything not already in the Company Portal goes through a security review, which takes up to five working days.

My laptop is slow or broken.

Restart it first; this fixes about a third of the problems we see. If that does not help, raise a ticket. Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner if a repair would cost more than half the price of a replacement.

The printer is not working.

Check that the printer shows as online on its own display, then remove and re-add it from the Company Portal. Card payment receipt printers are supported by the payment provider, not by IT; the support number is on the label underneath the device.

Wi-Fi and phones

Which Wi-Fi network should I use?

Company laptops join “Calderwick-Staff” automatically. Personal devices and visitors use “Calderwick-Guest”. The guest password changes on the first working day of each month and is kept at each clinic's front desk.

How much email can I store?

Each mailbox holds 50 GB. Messages older than two years move automatically to your online archive, which you can still search.

I have lost my work phone.

Call the Service Desk within one hour so that we can lock it. A replacement is normally sent to your clinic within two working days.
