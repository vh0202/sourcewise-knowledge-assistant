# Information Security Policy

Calderwick Health · IT and Information Governance · Version 5.1 · Effective 1 February 2026

## Scope

This policy applies to everyone who uses Calderwick Health systems or handles its information: employees, contractors, students on placement and temporary staff. It covers every device, account and record, on site or elsewhere.

## Passwords and sign-in

### Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. We recommend a passphrase of four or more unrelated words rather than a short, complicated password. You are not required to change your password on a schedule, but you must change it immediately if you suspect that someone else knows it. Never reuse a Calderwick password on another service, and never share it with anyone, including IT staff.

Every employee has a KeyVault password manager account. Store work passwords there, not in a notebook, a spreadsheet or your browser.

### Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, ClinicDesk, the People Portal and the CalderLink VPN. Use the authenticator app on your work or personal phone. Text-message codes are not supported. If you receive a sign-in prompt that you did not start, deny it and report it to the IT Service Desk straight away.

### Account lockout

An account locks after six failed sign-in attempts and unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. The IT Service Desk can unlock it sooner once it has verified your identity.

## Devices

### Laptops and phones

Company laptops are encrypted and are set to lock automatically after five minutes without activity. Do not change these settings. Install operating system and application updates within seven days of being prompted; updates that IT marks as critical must be installed within 48 hours.

Work email may be used on a personal phone only through the Company Portal app, which requires a six-digit PIN and allows IT to remove work data from the phone if it is lost. Patient records must never be opened on a personal device.

### Removable media

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. If you have a genuine need to move files physically, the IT Service Desk will issue an encrypted drive and record who holds it.

### Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop, phone or encrypted drive to the IT Service Desk within one hour of noticing, at any time of day. IT will lock the device remotely and erase it if it cannot be recovered. Reporting late is treated more seriously than the loss itself.

## Handling information

### Classification

All information belongs to one of four classes:

| Class | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Website content, published price lists | No restrictions |
| Internal | Rotas, policies, meeting notes | Share only with colleagues |
| Confidential | Contracts, salary data, financial forecasts | Share only with named people who need it; encrypt when sent outside the organisation |
| Patient-identifiable | Clinical notes, appointment histories, patient contact details | Keep in ClinicDesk; never send by ordinary email; access only for the care of that patient |

### Sending patient information

Patient-identifiable information is sent outside the organisation only through the secure message feature in ClinicDesk or by encrypted email, and only to a recipient whose address has been checked. Double-check the recipient before sending; misdirected email is our most common type of incident.

### Clear desk and clear screen

Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk. At the end of the day, lock away paper that contains confidential or patient-identifiable information. Confidential paper waste goes in the locked shredding bins, never in ordinary recycling.

## Email and phishing

Be suspicious of any message that creates urgency, asks for a password or payment, or contains an unexpected attachment or link. Report suspicious messages with the Report Phish button in your mail client, or forward them to security@example.com. Do not reply and do not forward them to colleagues. If you have clicked a link or entered your password, tell the IT Service Desk immediately; you will not be blamed for reporting quickly.

## Access to systems

Access is granted on the principle of least privilege: you receive the access your role needs and no more. Managers review who has access to their team's systems every quarter. When someone leaves, their accounts are disabled by 18:00 on their last working day. Looking at the record of a patient you are not involved in caring for is a disciplinary matter, even if you know the patient personally.

## Software and cloud services

Only software from the Company Portal may be installed without asking. Requests for anything else go to the IT Service Desk, which carries out a security review within five working days. Do not put Calderwick information into free online tools, including translation, file conversion and artificial intelligence services, unless IT has approved the tool for that class of information.

## Security incidents

A security incident is anything that may have exposed, altered or lost information: a misdirected email, a lost device, a suspicious sign-in, an unlocked filing cabinet, a virus warning. Report every suspected incident to the IT Service Desk within one hour of becoming aware of it. The Data Protection Officer assesses each report within 24 hours and decides whether the regulator and the people affected must be told, which the law requires within 72 hours for serious breaches.

## Training and compliance

Everyone completes Information Governance and Data Protection training within five working days of starting and once a year after that. Access to ClinicDesk is suspended if the training is more than 30 days overdue. Deliberate or repeated breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

Contact the IT Security Lead at security@example.com with questions about this policy.
