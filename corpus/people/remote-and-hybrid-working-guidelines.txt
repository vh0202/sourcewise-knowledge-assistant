REMOTE AND HYBRID WORKING GUIDELINES

Calderwick Health - People Team - Version 2.1 - Effective 1 September 2025

1. Who these guidelines are for

These guidelines apply to employees whose work does not have to be done in a clinic. Support office roles in Finance, People, Marketing, IT and Operations are eligible for hybrid working. Front-desk roles are not eligible because the work is done at the desk. Clinicians treat patients on site but may, by agreement with their clinic manager, take one half-day a fortnight at home for clinical administration.

2. How much time can be worked remotely

Eligible full-time employees may work from home for up to two days a week. The other three days are spent at the Eastmere support office or at a clinic. Each team chooses one anchor day a week when everyone is in the office together; your manager will tell you which day that is. New starters work on site every day for their first four weeks.

3. Core hours and availability

Whether at home or in the office, be available between 10:00 and 15:00, which are our core hours. Outside core hours you may arrange your working day to suit you, provided you work your contracted hours and your manager knows your pattern. Keep your calendar up to date and answer calls and messages within one hour during core hours.

4. Equipment

Calderwick Health provides a laptop, a headset and, on request, a monitor, keyboard and mouse for home use. You may also claim a one-off home office allowance of 150 pounds towards a desk or other furniture, paid through Expensa on production of a receipt. An office chair is supplied free of charge if your workstation self-assessment shows that you need one. Home broadband and electricity costs are not reimbursed.

5. Health and safety at home

Complete the online workstation self-assessment within two weeks of starting hybrid working and again whenever your home set-up changes. Report any work-related accident at home to your manager in the same way as an accident on site.

6. Information security at home

- Use only your company laptop for work. Do not use a personal or family computer.
- Connect through the CalderLink VPN before opening ClinicDesk or any shared drive.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away, even at home.
- Do not print patient-identifiable information at home.
- Take calls about patients somewhere you cannot be overheard.

7. Working from another country

Working from outside the United Kingdom is limited to 10 working days in a calendar year. It needs approval from both your head of department and the IT Security Lead at least four weeks before you travel, because some countries are blocked for access to patient systems. Requests are made on the Working Abroad form in the People Portal.

8. Meetings

Hybrid meetings start five minutes past the hour so that people can move between rooms. If any attendee is remote, everyone joins from their own laptop so that all participants are equally visible.

9. Changing or ending an arrangement

Hybrid working is not a contractual right. Your manager may ask you to attend on a home day with 48 hours' notice when the work requires it, and an arrangement can be ended with four weeks' notice if it is not working for the team.
