# Travel and Expense Policy

Calderwick Health · Finance · Version 3.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Purpose

Calderwick Health reimburses the reasonable cost of travel and other expenses that employees incur for work. This policy sets the limits, explains how to claim and says who approves what. Spend the organisation's money as carefully as you would your own.

## Travel

### Rail and air

Book standard class rail travel through the Finance travel desk at least seven days ahead whenever you can. First class is allowed only when it is cheaper than the standard fare available at the time of booking. Flights are economy class and need approval from a head of department before booking.

### Driving

If you use your own car for work journeys, claim 45p per mile. Travel between your home and your normal clinic is commuting and cannot be claimed. When you travel from home to a different clinic, claim only the miles beyond your usual commute. Your insurance must cover business use; Finance may ask to see the certificate. Bicycle journeys are claimed at 20p per mile.

### Taxis

Taxis are for exceptional cases: when no public transport is available, when you are carrying clinic equipment, or when you are travelling after 21:00. Note the reason on the claim.

### Parking and tolls

Parking and toll charges incurred on a work journey are reimbursed at cost. Parking fines and speeding penalties are never reimbursed.

## Accommodation and meals

### Hotels

Hotel rooms are booked through the Finance travel desk. The limit is £120 a night, including breakfast, or £165 a night in London. If nothing is available within the limit, ask Finance to approve an exception before you book.

### Meals while travelling

When work takes you away from your normal clinic for more than five hours you may claim a meal, and when you stay overnight you may claim an evening meal. The limits are:

| Meal | Limit |
|---|---|
| Breakfast (only if not included in the room rate) | £8 |
| Lunch | £12 |
| Evening meal | £25 |

Alcohol is not reimbursed.

### Hospitality

Entertaining referrers or other business contacts must be approved in advance by a head of department and is limited to £40 a head, including drinks.

## What we do not reimburse

- alcohol, minibar charges and in-room entertainment
- fines and penalties of any kind
- travel costs for a partner or family member
- clothing, except items required by a specific risk assessment
- home broadband and personal mobile phone contracts

## Making a claim

### Receipts

Submit an itemised receipt for every item over £10. A card statement alone is not a receipt. If a receipt is lost, your line manager must confirm the expense in writing, and no more than two lost-receipt claims are accepted per person per year.

### Deadline

Enter claims in Expensa within 30 days of the date of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not paid unless the Finance Director agrees there was a good reason for the delay.

### When you are paid

Claims approved by the 10th of the month are reimbursed with your salary on the 25th. Claims approved after the 10th are paid the following month.

## Approval limits

| Value of a single claim | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to £250 | Line manager |
| £250.01 to £1,000 | Head of department |
| Over £1,000 | Finance Director |

Nobody approves their own claim, and a manager's claim goes to their own line manager.

## Company payment cards

Clinic managers and heads of department hold a company payment card with a monthly limit of £1,500. Cardholders upload receipts to Expensa and reconcile their statement within five working days of the month end. A card is suspended if two statements in a row are reconciled late. Cards must not be used for personal spending, even if you intend to repay it.

## Training-related travel

Travel to a course funded from your development budget is claimed under this policy and does not count against the budget itself.
