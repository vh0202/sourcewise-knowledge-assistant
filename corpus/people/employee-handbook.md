# Employee Handbook

Calderwick Health · People Team · Version 4.2 · Effective 1 March 2026

This handbook explains how we work at Calderwick Health and what you can expect from us as an employer. It applies to everyone employed by Calderwick Health across our five clinics and the Eastmere support office. Where your contract of employment says something different, your contract takes precedence.

## Working hours

### Standard hours

Full-time employees work 37.5 hours a week. Clinics open to patients from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 14:00 on Saturdays, so clinical and front-desk staff work to a rota published in RotaBoard at least four weeks in advance. Support office staff normally work Monday to Friday between 08:30 and 17:30.

### Breaks

Anyone working a shift longer than six hours takes an unpaid break of at least 30 minutes, away from the desk or treatment room. Clinicians have a protected 15-minute administration slot after every fourth patient.

### Overtime and time off in lieu

Overtime must be agreed with your line manager before it is worked. Hours worked beyond your contracted week are normally repaid as time off in lieu, which must be taken within eight weeks. Overtime is paid, at your normal hourly rate, only when a clinic manager has confirmed that time off in lieu is not practical.

### Swapping shifts

You may swap a shift with a colleague in the same role provided your clinic manager approves the swap in RotaBoard at least 48 hours before the shift starts.

## Leave

### Annual leave

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave each year, plus the eight public holidays. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement pro rata to their contracted hours. After five years of continuous service your entitlement rises to 27 days, and after ten years to 30 days.

Request leave in RotaBoard. As a rule, give notice of at least twice the length of the leave you are asking for; for example, request one week off at least two weeks in advance. Requests for more than ten consecutive working days need approval from your head of department as well as your line manager.

You may carry over up to five unused days into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost. We do not pay in lieu of untaken leave except when you leave the company.

### Buying additional leave

Once a year, in November, you can apply to buy up to five extra days of leave for the following year. The cost is deducted from your salary in twelve equal instalments.

### Sickness absence

If you are too unwell to work, telephone your line manager before 08:00 on the first day of absence, or at least one hour before your shift starts if it begins earlier. A text message or email is not enough on the first day. Keep your manager updated on each further day unless you have agreed otherwise.

For absences of up to seven calendar days you complete a self-certification form on your return. For longer absences you need a fit note from your doctor. Once you have passed probation, Calderwick Health pays company sick pay at full salary for up to 10 working days in any rolling 12-month period; after that, statutory sick pay applies. A return-to-work conversation is held after every absence, and a formal absence review is triggered by three separate absences in six months.

### Parental leave

Employees with at least one year of service at the expected week of childbirth or placement receive enhanced maternity or adoption pay of 16 weeks at full pay. Partners are entitled to two weeks of paternity or partner leave at full pay. Tell the People Team about a pregnancy or adoption match at least 15 weeks before the expected date so that cover can be planned.

### Compassionate leave

You may take up to five days of paid compassionate leave following the death or serious illness of a close relative or dependant. Your line manager can agree additional unpaid leave.

### Study leave

Clinicians receive three paid study days a year for continuing professional development. Study days do not carry over.

## Probation and notice

New employees serve a probation period of six months. Your manager will hold a review with you at three months and again before the end of the period, and probation can be extended once by up to three months. During probation either side may end employment with one week's notice. After probation the notice period is one month for most roles and three months for clinic managers and heads of department.

## Pay and benefits

### Pay day

Salaries are paid by bank transfer on the 25th of each month, or on the last working day before it if the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Payslips are published in the People Portal two days before pay day.

### Pension

You are enrolled in the company pension scheme from your first day. Calderwick Health contributes 5% of your basic salary and you contribute a minimum of 4%. You can raise your own contribution at any time through the People Portal.

### Professional fees and development

We reimburse the annual registration fee that clinicians pay to their professional regulator. Each clinician has a development budget of £600 a year, and each non-clinical employee £300 a year, for courses agreed with their manager.

### Staff treatment discount

Employees and their immediate family receive 50% off physiotherapy sessions at any Calderwick clinic, subject to availability.

## Conduct at work

### Dress and identification

Clinical staff wear the navy Calderwick tunic or polo shirt provided. Front-desk staff wear the grey polo shirt provided with dark trousers or a skirt. Everyone wears their photo ID badge at chest height while on clinic premises. Closed-toe shoes are required in treatment areas.

### Gifts and hospitality

You may accept a small gift from a patient, such as chocolates or flowers, if it is worth £25 or less. Anything worth more must be politely declined, and every gift, accepted or declined, is recorded in the gifts register held by your clinic manager. Cash gifts are never accepted.

### Social media

Never post anything that could identify a patient, even with good intentions. Do not present personal opinions as the views of Calderwick Health. Requests from journalists go to the Communications Lead.

### Raising a concern

If you see something that puts patients, colleagues or the organisation at risk, speak to your manager or use the confidential Speak Up form in the People Portal. Concerns raised in good faith will never count against you.

## Leaving Calderwick Health

Resign in writing to your line manager. On or before your last working day you return your ID badge, uniform, laptop and any keys to your clinic manager. Untaken annual leave is paid with your final salary; leave taken in excess of your accrued entitlement is deducted from it. An exit conversation with the People Team is offered to every leaver.
