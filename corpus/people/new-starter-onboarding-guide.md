# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Calderwick Health · People Team · Updated February 2026

Welcome to Calderwick Health. This guide covers what happens before you start, during your first week and over your first three months. Your line manager and your buddy will go through it with you.

## Before your first day

We need three things from you at least five working days before you start: your signed contract, proof of your right to work, and your bank details for payroll. Patient-facing roles also require an enhanced background check, which we apply for as soon as you accept the offer; you cannot work unsupervised with patients until it has cleared.

Your manager will email you a first-week timetable on the Thursday before you start. If you have not received it by then, contact the People Team.

## Your first day

Everyone starts at the Eastmere support office, whichever clinic they will be based at. Arrive at 09:30 and ask for the People Team at reception. During the morning you will:

- have your photograph taken and collect your ID badge
- collect your laptop and, if your role needs one, a mobile phone
- sign in to your email, RotaBoard and the People Portal with IT
- meet your buddy, who will take you to lunch at the company's expense

In the afternoon you travel to your home clinic, meet the team and walk through the fire exits and first-aid points with the clinic manager.

## Your first week

### Mandatory training

Some training must be finished before you handle patient information or work alone:

| Course | Who | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Information Governance and Data Protection | Everyone | Within 5 working days of starting |
| Fire Safety | Everyone | Within 30 days of starting |
| Basic Life Support | Patient-facing staff | Within 30 days of starting |
| Safeguarding Adults and Children, Level 2 | Patient-facing staff | Within 30 days of starting |
| Manual Handling | Clinicians | Within 60 days of starting |

All courses are in the Learning Hub. Information Governance is repeated every year; the others every two years.

### Learning the systems

Front-desk staff complete the ClinicDesk booking course and then work two shadow shifts alongside an experienced receptionist before running a desk alone. Clinicians complete ClinicDesk clinical-notes training and observe at least three patient sessions before starting their own list. Your first week's list is booked at half capacity.

### Uniform

Clinical and front-desk staff receive three polo shirts or tunics in their first week. Replacements are issued free once a year; extra items can be bought at cost.

## Your buddy

Your buddy is a colleague from your clinic, not your manager, who has been with us for at least a year. They are your first port of call for everyday questions for your first eight weeks, and you will have a 20-minute check-in together each week.

## Checkpoints in your first three months

- **30 days:** a conversation with your manager about how you are settling in and whether training is on track.
- **60 days:** a review of your objectives; clinicians also have a notes audit of five patient records.
- **90 days:** your formal three-month probation review.

## Getting paid for the first time

Payroll closes on the 10th of each month. If you start on or before the 10th you are paid on the 25th of the same month. If you start after the 10th, your first pay arrives on the 25th of the following month and includes the days worked in your first month. Bring your P45 from your previous employer if you have one.

- Laptop, accounts and passwords: IT Service Desk, it-servicedesk@example.com
